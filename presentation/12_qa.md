## Anticipated questions (rehearsal)

- *"Why did you drop religion / race?"* → Slide 7 — and we measured the cost: +0.7pt of pure bias.
- *"How did you know `internal_code` was a leak and not just a great feature?"* → Slide 4 — the opinion-probe: it correlated with real signal in train but was scrambled in test.
- *"Why is your CV score higher than your leaderboard score?"* → Slide 9 — distribution shift (May 2026 is harder), not overfitting; our gap is small and stable.
- *"Why this model vs alternatives?"* → Slide 8 — gradient-boosted trees dominate tabular; CatBoost handled the opinion column best.
- *"Why didn't you just submit more to climb the public board?"* → Slide 9/10 — the public split is 40% and too noisy to resolve our differences; chasing it overfits the part that doesn't count.
- *"What was the biggest challenge?"* → Slide 10 — forensics, not modeling.
- *"How would you deploy this?"* → Slide 11 — calibration, cost-sensitive thresholds, drift + fairness monitoring.
